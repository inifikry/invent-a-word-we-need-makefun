# Invent a Word We Need

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Invent a Word We Need](https://make.fun/c/u-deranalabs-invent-a-word-we-need-17a020e0) ($5, deadline 27 Nov 2026 16:55 WIB).

## The word

> **wavemiss (n.) — the sinking moment you realize the wave you just returned was meant for someone behind you.**

English has no word for that small public embarrassment: you wave back, warmly, and then see the hand was aimed at the person behind you. *Wavemiss* names it in one word — *wave* + *miss* — sayable, spellable, and instantly recognizable.
